VoiceBill — Voice-Based Billing App

Tagline: Speak. Calculate. Bill.

1. Project Overview

VoiceBill is a mobile app concept designed to simplify billing for small shop owners. It allows shopkeepers to add products to a bill using voice commands, review item details, and calculate the total amount.

2. Problem Statement

Small shop owners often enter product names, quantities, and prices manually when preparing customer bills. This process can be time-consuming, especially during busy hours.

3. Proposed Solution

VoiceBill introduces voice-based product entry to make billing faster and easier. Shopkeepers can speak product details, review the recognized information, and generate a bill after confirming the entries.

4. Target Users

- Small grocery shop owners
- Local retail shopkeepers
- Small business owners

5. Key Features

- Voice-based product entry
- Manual correction of recognized product details
- Automatic total calculation
- Bill preview before generation
- Sales history

6. Primary User Screens

1. Welcome Screen: Introduces VoiceBill and provides access to the app.
2. Billing Dashboard: Provides access to new billing and previous bills.
3. Voice Input: Allows users to enter product names, quantities, and prices using voice.
4. Bill Preview: Displays products, quantities, prices, and the grand total.
5. Sales History: Displays previously generated bills and their amounts.

7. Navigation Flow

Welcome Screen → Billing Dashboard → Voice Input → Bill Preview → Generate Bill.

The Billing Dashboard also provides access to Sales History.

8. Example Use Case

A shopkeeper says, "Add two Good Day biscuits at 10 rupees each."

The app recognizes the product details and displays:

- Product: Good Day Biscuits
- Quantity: 2
- Price per item: ₹10
- Total: ₹20

The shopkeeper reviews the item and confirms it before generating the bill.

9. Design Goal

The interface will use large buttons, readable text, clear labels, and minimal navigation steps to support quick billing in busy retail environments.

10. Project Scope

This submission focuses on the app concept, primary screens, and navigation flow. Voice recognition, bill generation, and other functional features are proposed capabilities and are not claimed to be implemented.
